## Key Verification Notes & Student Advice

Before I sign off, here are some important notes and pieces of advice you should keep in mind during your search:

- Price & Deposit Disclaimer: All listed prices, sharing breakdowns, and security deposits are indicative or based on direct student responses. Actual prices may vary depending on room size, ventilation, attached/common washrooms, and food plans. Please contact the PG management directly to verify exact terms before paying any advance.
- Sri Guru PG / Guru Shree PG (Rajajinagar): Both listings refer to the same active property located at #92/12, 4th Cross, SSI Area, Dr. Rajkumar Road. Form responses confirm operational pricing (1-share ₹7k, 2-share ₹6.5k, 3-share ₹6k, 4-share ₹5.5k with washing machine available).
- SBT Kuruba Community Hostel (Mallya Hospital / UB City): Community/subsidized student accommodation (~1.0 km walkable from UVCE). Features dormitory/multi-sharing (>5 sharing) starting at ₹1,500/month per person. Check admission and community criteria prior to visit.
- Ashraya Ladies PG (Shanti Nagar): Verified student rates report 1-sharing at ₹13,000, 2-sharing at ₹9,500–₹10,000, and 3-sharing at ₹8,000 behind BTS Garage, 1st F Cross.
- GVR Comforts PG (Cubbonpete): Located at 9th Cross Cubbonpete (~0.5 km walkable to UVCE). 4-sharing is ₹7,500 with washing machine facility on site.
- Gravity Ladies PG (Vijayanagar): Newly added PG at 8th Main, Vijayanagar near Purple Line Metro (~7.5 km). Verified rates: 1-share ₹12k, 2-share ₹11k, 3-share ₹8k, 5-share ₹7.5k.
- Zen Star PG 2 & Nisarga Men's PG: Newly added options. Zen Star PG 2 is near St. John's Hospital (Koramangala/Madiwala, ~8.0 km) with ₹5.5k–₹7.5k sharing rates. Nisarga Men's PG is at Rangaswamy Complex, Rajajinagar (~4.0 km) with ₹6.8k–₹9k sharing rates.
- Co-ed / Policy Clarification: PADHAROSA PG for Girls and Shree Kalika PG for Ladies have mixed/co-ed reviews despite their names — confirm floor segregation and policies directly.
- Restricted Admission: Maheshwari Girls Hostel and Sri Madhwa Yuvaka Sangha have community- or college-restricted admission criteria — check eligibility before visiting.
- Food Inclusions: Oak Woods Ladies PG and The White Casa Women PG reviews indicate food is not included on-site (students rely on tiffin or local messes).
- Due Diligence Checklist: Before paying any token amount, personally inspect: water pressure, room ventilation, electricity billing mode (sub-meter per room vs flat-rate), washing machine token fees, and notice period duration (standard is 30 days).



Best of luck finding your new space!
